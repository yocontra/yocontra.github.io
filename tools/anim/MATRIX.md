# Out West animation matrix

Every character state in every mode, with how it is animated and how it was verified. Paths are
relative to `src/experiments/out-west/` unless they start with `tools/`.

`tools/anim-audit.ts` checks the states it can reach, on the production build with the harness
hooks kept (`PUBLIC_OW_PERF_HARNESS=1`; the deploy never sets it). For each state it drives the
state, captures a frame sequence from the state's camera and checks three things:

- a clip or a procedural layer is active (`scene/anim-probe.ts`);
- the key bones are off the bind pose (no T-pose), off idle where the state should change the
  pose, and moving between frames where they should move;
- `__owTPose` stays 0.

Sequences are written to `.playwright-mcp/anim-audit/<section>/<state>/` (frames plus
`sheet.png`), with a summary in `.playwright-mcp/anim-audit/report.json`. Rerun with
`bun tools/anim-audit.ts`.

**Legend**

- **Status:** ✅ implemented and verified · ⚠️ weak · ❌ missing · n/a (no character in that view).
- **Before** is the status at `fe5841a`; **Status** is the status now.
- **Actors:** P player · O outlaw · R PvP opponent or ghost · F townsfolk · A animals · FL Frontier local player · FR Frontier remote rider · FC far crowd (VAT) · VM first-person view model.
- **Views:** duel = duel camera (OTS, back to back, standoff) · kill = kill cam · hub = hub or Outfitter · line = `#lineup` audit stage · tps = Frontier third person · fps = first person · top = top-down.
- **LOD:** near = skinned and full rate; mid = skinned at 15 Hz (townsfolk, animals, Frontier riders beyond 12 m); far = VAT crowd.
- **Blend:** fade-in / fade-out in seconds (`play(name, fade)`). "proc" means a procedural layer; its own easing is given.
- **Audit names:** `duel/<state>` is the lineup stage (`duel/anim-stage.ts`), `live/<phase>` is a played Quick Draw against the talker, and `frontier/<state>` is Frontier with 12 bots.

The new layers cost almost nothing when idle. `GestureLayer.apply` returns after a few
multiplies when nothing is on, which covers every townsfolk. While a beat or a held layer is on
(two duellists at most), it does one subtree world-matrix update and about ten bone turns. It
stands down under the draw, so the call frame is the DrawLayer's alone. Townsfolk and animals
were already throttled (15–30 Hz, staggered, skin-throttled). The probe and the stage are
tree-shaken from the deploy (`PROBE` is false unless dev or `PUBLIC_OW_PERF_HARNESS=1`).
